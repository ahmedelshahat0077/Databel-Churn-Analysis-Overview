# 📊 Databel Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn for **Databel** to identify why customers leave and provide data-driven solutions to lower churn and protect business revenue.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow
* **Tools Used:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX.
* **Process:** 
  1. Cleaned & transformed raw customer data using **Power Query**.
  2. Created dynamic churn metrics & DAX measures.
  3. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for executive decision-making.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Problems Identified
* **High Churn Rate:** Overall churn stands at **26.86%** (1,796 out of 6,687 customers lost).
* **Competitor Pressure:** **44.8%** of churned customers left due to competitor pricing and device offers.
* **Contract Risk:** Month-to-Month contracts showed significantly higher churn than long-term plans.
* **Demographic Impact:** Senior Citizens had a high **40% churn rate**, indicating fit/support issues.
* **Geographic Risk:** California showed the highest concentration of churned users.

---

## 💡 Strategic Solutions & Recommendations
1. **Competitor Counter-Strategy:** Launch price-matching incentives and device upgrade perks.
2. **Contract Migration:** Offer discounts to convert Month-to-Month users into yearly contracts.
3. **Senior Care Packages:** Create simplified plans and dedicated customer support for senior citizens.
4. **Targeted Campaigns:** Run localized retention deals in high-churn states like California.
5. **Early Warning System:** Monitor support call frequency as an indicator for churn risk.
